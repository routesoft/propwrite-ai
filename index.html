Questions agents ask us

Does RouteSoft replace my MLS software? +

No. RouteSoft writes the copy — the description, caption, and email — that you paste into your existing MLS, CRM, or social scheduler. It doesn't list or syndicate properties.

Will the listing copy sound like a generic AI wrote it? +

RouteSoft uses the specific features and vibe you enter rather than a fixed template, so the output reflects the property and tone you choose. You can always edit before publishing.

Can I edit the generated copy before I publish it? +

Yes. Every output is plain, editable text you can copy into your MLS, social scheduler, or email tool and adjust however you like.

How many listings can I generate? +

There's no fixed cap during your trial. Paid plans and usage limits are outlined on the pricing page once you sign up.
